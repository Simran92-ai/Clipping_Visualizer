GIS Map Visualization

A fictional map (roads, river, buildings, lake, park, boundaries, landmarks). Only what falls inside the clipping window is drawn. Coordinates are sent to Flask, which clips lines with Cohen-Sutherland and polygons with Sutherland-Hodgman.
