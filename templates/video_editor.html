Clipping in video editing

Titles, logos and clips often move or zoom partly beyond the frame (slide-in titles, pan & zoom). The editor clips each layer against the frame so that only pixels inside the output area are rendered, which saves processing time. Rectangles, circles and picture shapes are clipped as polygons (Sutherland-Hodgman); text is clipped to the frame rectangle. Click an object to see its status.
