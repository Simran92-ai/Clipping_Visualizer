Window-Based Image Viewer

Upload any image (or use the built-in sample). The blue rectangle is the fixed viewing window. Zoom and pan the image – only the part inside the window is displayed; the rest is clipped (shown faintly outside the window).

Drag with the mouse to pan • mouse wheel to zoom.

What is happening?

The image rectangle is clipped against the viewing window. Only the intersection is passed to the drawing routine, so portions outside the window are never rendered or displayed. This is why large, high-resolution images can be browsed smoothly.
